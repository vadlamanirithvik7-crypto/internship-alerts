THE ENGINEERING

A product you can use.
A system you can inspect.

Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · ONNX embeddings · GitHub Actions

01 / COLLECT

Bring the sources together

Company job boards and community feeds flow through a scheduled, concurrent poller. Canonical URLs deduplicate repeated listings; original records preserve provenance.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Connect experience to roles

A locally executed embedding model compares resume and job text. Semantic relevance combines with exact skills and freshness; explicit preferences remain filters.

03 / EXPLAIN

Show the supporting evidence

Each comparison links back to excerpts from the resume and posting. Optional language-model explanations must pass quote validation, with source excerpts as a fallback.

04 / DELIVER

Keep progress durable

A persistent outbox retries failed notifications. Incremental commits protect ingestion progress, and source observations reveal degraded coverage.
